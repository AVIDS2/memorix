# Research Claim Ledger

No entry below is a current product claim. A claim moves beyond `Unproven` only
after a frozen, independently reviewable study satisfies its stated gate.

| ID | Candidate claim | Required evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Normal Memorix project context improves dependent coding-task correctness over no memory. | Preregistered private-oracle cases, matched tool/model budgets, canonical-information controls, complete retained rows, and a paired analysis across held-out repositories. | Unproven |

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- A public fixture is not a private-oracle task.
- A product unit test is not an agent outcome study.
- A broken adapter is infrastructure failure, not a zero for a competing system.
- A successful local smoke does not prove generalization.
- A single model or repository cannot support a cross-model or cross-agent claim.
- Results from Memorix's own repository cannot establish general coding-agent value.
